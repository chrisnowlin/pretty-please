[IMAGE: Fraction Circle Diagram]
Description: A circle divided into 4 equal parts, with 1 part shaded
Caption: Figure 1 - One-fourth (1/4) of a circle
Use: Show students how 1/4 represents one part out of four equal parts

[IMAGE: Fraction Bar Set]
Description: Rectangular bars showing halves, thirds, fourths, and eighths
Caption: Figure 2 - Comparing fraction bars

[IMAGE: Number Line with Fractions]
Description: A number line from 0 to 1 with marks at 1/4, 1/2, and 3/4
Caption: Figure 3 - Fractions on a number line
Use: Show the relationship between fractions and whole numbers

[IMAGE: Pizza Fractions]
Description: A pizza divided into 8 slices, with 3 slices shown separately
Caption: Figure 4 - 3/8 of a pizza
Use: Real-world example of fractions using food

[IMAGE: Fraction Wall Chart]
Description: A visual chart showing equivalent fractions aligned vertically
Rows show: 1 whole, halves, thirds, fourths, sixths, eighths
Caption: Figure 5 - Fraction wall showing equivalent fractions
Use: Help students see which fractions are equal in size

Teaching Tips for Using Visual Aids:

1. Always point to the visual while explaining
2. Have students create their own visual models
3. Use color coding to highlight different fractions
4. Display visuals prominently in the classroom
5. Reference visuals during practice problems

Visual Learning Benefits:
- Makes abstract concepts concrete
- Helps visual learners understand better
- Provides a reference for problem-solving
- Supports students who struggle with numbers alone
- Makes math more engaging and accessible

Remember: Visual models are essential tools for teaching fractions to elementary students!
